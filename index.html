About Company

Professional Automotive Solutions in Papua New Guinea

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED is a professional automotive service company based in Lae, Papua New Guinea.


We provide vehicle repair and maintenance, automotive parts supply, heavy equipment parts support, hydraulic hose services, quality used vehicle sales, fleet support, and roadside assistance.
