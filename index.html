About Company

Professional Automotive Solutions in Papua New Guinea

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED is a professional automotive service company based in Lae, Papua New Guinea.


We specialize in vehicle repair and maintenance, body paint services, automotive spare parts supply, heavy equipment parts support, and quality used vehicle sales.


Our company is committed to delivering reliable service, professional workmanship, and long-term support for both individual customers and commercial clients across Papua New Guinea.

Our Services

Comprehensive automotive and engineering solutions

Vehicle Repair

Professional diagnostics, engine repair, brake service, suspension repair, electrical troubleshooting, and scheduled maintenance.

Body Paint & Panel Work

Quality body repair, accident restoration, repainting, polishing, and vehicle appearance enhancement.

Auto Parts Supply

Reliable spare parts, filters, lubricants, batteries, tires, and automotive accessories for major vehicle brands.

Heavy Equipment Parts

Components and spare parts support for excavators, loaders, industrial vehicles, and construction equipment.

Quality Used Vehicles

Inspection-supported quality used vehicle sales for private, commercial, and project use.
