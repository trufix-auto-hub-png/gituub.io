About Company

Professional Automotive Solutions in Papua New Guinea

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED is a professional automotive service company based in Lae, Papua New Guinea.


We provide vehicle repair and maintenance, body paint services, automotive parts supply, heavy equipment parts support, hydraulic hose services, quality used vehicle sales, and roadside assistance.


Our company is committed to reliable service, professional workmanship, transparent communication, safety-focused operations, and long-term support for individual customers, business clients, fleet operators, construction companies, and project customers.

Vehicle Repair

Professional repair and maintenance solutions

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED provides professional vehicle repair and maintenance services for private customers, commercial operators, and fleet vehicles in Papua New Guinea.


Our workshop services include engine service, diagnostics, brake service, suspension and steering repair, electrical troubleshooting, air-conditioning service, cooling system repair, fast maintenance, body paint, and panel work.


We focus on reliable workmanship, efficient repair turnaround, practical maintenance support, and accountable service for daily vehicle operations.

Parts Supply

Automotive spare parts and heavy equipment components

We supply automotive spare parts and heavy equipment components for commercial, industrial, and fleet operations across Papua New Guinea.


Our automotive parts support includes engine parts, electrical parts, brake parts, suspension parts, filters, lubricants, batteries, belts, hoses, cooling system components, and common wear items.


Our heavy equipment parts support includes components for excavators, loaders, compact machinery, hydraulic systems, construction equipment, hydraulic hoses, filters, wear parts, and engine or system components.


We focus on reliability, availability, durability, and operational efficiency for customers who depend on vehicles and equipment every day.
