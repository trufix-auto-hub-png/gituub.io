Company Overview

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED is a registered automotive service company based in Lae, Morobe Province, Papua New Guinea. We provide professional vehicle repair, body paint services, automotive parts supply, engineering vehicle parts supply, and quality used vehicle sales.

We serve individual customers, corporate clients, fleet operators, construction contractors, logistics companies, and project clients with reliable, transparent, and responsive service.

Our Main Business Areas

Vehicle Repair & Body Paint

Engine repair, diagnostics, brake service, suspension repair, transmission service, air-conditioning service, body repair, panel beating, and spray painting.

Automotive Parts Supply

Supply of quality automotive spare parts including filters, oils, batteries, engine parts, brake parts, suspension parts, electrical parts, belts, hoses, and vehicle accessories.

Engineering Vehicle Parts

Supply of excavator parts, loader parts, hydraulic hoses, filters, wear parts, undercarriage components, and construction machinery parts.

Quality Used Cars

High-quality used vehicle sales. Vehicles are inspected and checked before sale, suitable for personal, commercial, corporate, and fleet use.
