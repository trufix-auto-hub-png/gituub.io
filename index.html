Company Overview

Professional Automotive Solutions in Papua New Guinea

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED is a professional automotive service company based in Lae, Papua New Guinea. We provide vehicle repair and maintenance, body paint services, automotive spare parts supply, engineering vehicle parts, and quality used vehicle sales.


We serve individual customers, corporate clients, fleet operators, construction contractors, and project customers with reliable service, professional workmanship, quality parts, and responsive support.

Our Business Areas

Four main business divisions supporting customers in Lae and PNG

Vehicle Repair & Body Paint

Complete vehicle maintenance, diagnostics, engine repair, brake service, suspension repair, air-conditioning service, panel beating, and professional spray painting.

Automotive Parts

Supply of high-quality automotive spare parts including filters, oils, batteries, brake parts, suspension parts, belts, hoses, and electrical components.

Engineering Vehicle Parts

Supply of engineering and heavy equipment parts for excavators, loaders, trucks, hydraulic systems, and construction machinery.

Quality Used Cars

Inspected and reliable used vehicle sales for personal, commercial, corporate, and fleet customers.
