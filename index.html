Company Overview

Professional Automotive Solutions in Papua New Guinea

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED is a professional automotive service company based in Lae, Papua New Guinea. We specialize in vehicle repair and maintenance, body paint services, automotive spare parts supply, engineering vehicle parts, and quality used vehicle sales. Our company is committed to delivering reliable service, professional workmanship, high-quality parts, and fast response support for both individual and business customers.
