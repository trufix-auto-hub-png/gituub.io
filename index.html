About Company

Professional Automotive Solutions in Papua New Guinea

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED is a professional automotive service company based in Lae, Papua New Guinea.


We provide vehicle repair and maintenance, body paint services, automotive parts supply, heavy equipment parts support, and quality used vehicle sales.


Our company is committed to reliable service, professional workmanship, transparent communication, and long-term support for individual customers, business clients, fleet operators, construction companies, and project customers.

Vehicle Repair

Professional repair and maintenance solutions

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED provides professional vehicle repair and maintenance services for private customers, commercial operators, and fleet vehicles in Papua New Guinea.


Our workshop services include diagnostics, engine repair, brake service, suspension repair, electrical troubleshooting, air-conditioning service, body paint, and panel work.


We focus on reliable workmanship, efficient repair turnaround, and practical support for daily vehicle operations.

Parts Supply

Automotive and heavy equipment parts support

We supply automotive spare parts and heavy equipment components for commercial, industrial, and fleet operations across Papua New Guinea.


Our parts support includes filters, lubricants, batteries, belts, hoses, suspension components, engine parts, and construction equipment components.


We support major vehicle and equipment brands commonly used in PNG, with a focus on reliability, availability, and operational efficiency.

Quality Used Vehicles

Reliable vehicles for private and commercial customers

TRUFIX AUTO HUB LIMITED provides inspected and reliable used vehicles for private customers, business operators, and fleet buyers.


We focus on vehicle quality, practical performance, and long-term customer value.
